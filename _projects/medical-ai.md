## Project Overview

Led the development and commercialization of OmniHealth medical AI applications, from technical development to business implementation, creating a comprehensive ecosystem for medical AI services.

## Core Products

### Medical Question-Answering Plugin
- **Platform**: Developed "医问灵通" plugin for Baidu's Wenxin platform
- **Functionality**: Medical knowledge Q&A, diagnostic suggestions, and treatment guidance
- **Recognition**: CCF-BDCI National Third Prize (2023.11)

### Multi-modal Medical AI Model
- **Model**: OmniHealth Medical Monkey - advanced multi-modal medical AI system
- **Capabilities**: Multi-agent interaction framework and mobile app development
- **Integration**: Seamless integration of AI consultation, report interpretation, and health management

### Mobile Application
- **Platform**: OmniHealth mobile app with core functionality
- **Features**: AI consultation, report analysis, health record management
- **User Base**: Served 5000+ users across multiple scenarios
